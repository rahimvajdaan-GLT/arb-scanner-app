ABOUT

A simpler way to understand arbitrage

Kaka Arb is a sports-odds comparison tool designed to surface mathematical arbitrage candidates and make the stake calculation easy to inspect.

What we focus on

The product focuses on three things: clear odds comparison, transparent maths and a fast interface. We deliberately separate true arbitrage from ordinary value comparisons so users can see which rows meet the mathematical condition and which do not.

Built for Australian users

The interface uses Australian dollars and is designed around decimal odds. Users should always verify each bookmaker price, market definition and settlement rule before acting.

What Kaka Arb is not

Kaka Arb is not a bookmaker and does not place bets. It does not guarantee profit. Real-world execution can be affected by price movement, stake restrictions, account limits, voided markets and differing rules.
