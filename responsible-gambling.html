RESPONSIBLE USE

Use Kaka Arb responsibly

Kaka Arb is a calculation and comparison tool. It should never be used as a reason to bet more than you can afford to lose.

Set hard limits

Decide your maximum bankroll, maximum stake per event and maximum time spent before you start. Do not increase stakes to chase losses or compensate for an opportunity that disappeared.

Verify every market

Check the exact event, selection, line, overtime treatment, void rules and accepted stake before placing the second side. A quoted arbitrage can fail if either side changes.

Remember execution risk

Bookmakers can move prices, limit stakes, reject wagers, void errors or settle markets differently. A mathematical edge is not the same as guaranteed cash profit.

Take breaks

If betting stops being recreational, causes stress, creates financial pressure or feels difficult to control, stop using the tool and seek support from a qualified local gambling-help service.

Kaka Arb does not provide financial advice, betting advice or a guarantee of returns.
