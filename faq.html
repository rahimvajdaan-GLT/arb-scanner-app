FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is sports arbitrage?

It is a situation where the best quoted odds across different bookmakers allow every outcome to be covered with a theoretical positive return.

What is a surebet?

“Surebet” is a common name for a mathematical arbitrage setup. The term should not be interpreted as a real-world guarantee because execution risks still exist.

Does Top Notch Arb place bets for me?

No. Top Notch Arb compares prices and calculates stake splits. You must verify and place any wagers yourself.

What does TRUE ARB mean?

It means the quoted prices satisfy the arbitrage formula in the scanner. You should still verify the event, market, odds and bookmaker rules.

What does VALUE mean?

It is an informational comparison that does not satisfy the same guaranteed-return condition. Value rows can lose money.

Why can an arbitrage disappear?

Bookmaker prices move constantly. A profitable combination can vanish if one side changes before both wagers are accepted.

Can bookmakers limit stakes?

Yes. Operators can apply stake limits, reject wagers or restrict accounts according to their terms and risk policies.

Which currency is used?

Top Notch Arb displays financial figures in Australian dollars (AUD).

Is Top Notch Arb a bookmaker?

No. It is an independent odds-comparison and calculation tool.

Is arbitrage legal?

Laws and bookmaker terms vary by location and operator. Users should check the rules that apply to them before wagering.
