How the calculation works

For decimal odds, an arbitrage exists when the sum of the inverse odds is below 1.

2-way: 1 / Odds A + 1 / Odds B < 1 3-way: 1 / Home + 1 / Draw + 1 / Away < 1
Important

This app shows mathematical outcomes only. Real-world results can differ because odds move, stakes can be rejected or limited, fees may apply, and bookmaker settlement rules may not match.
