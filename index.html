Common questions

What is sports arbitrage?

It is a pricing situation where backing every mutually exclusive outcome at different quoted odds produces a theoretical return greater than the combined stake.

Does Kaka Arb guarantee profit?

No. The maths can show a theoretical arbitrage at quoted odds, but real-world risks include price changes, stake limits, rejected bets, voids and different settlement rules.

Which currency does Kaka Arb use?

The interface displays total stake, payouts and profit in Australian dollars (AUD).
