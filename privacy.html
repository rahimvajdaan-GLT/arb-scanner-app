Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

Information we process

Top Notch Arb may process technical request data required to operate the website and API. If login is enabled, authentication requests include the email address supplied by the user. Passwords are checked server-side and are not intentionally stored in browser storage.

Cookies

The account area uses a secure HTTP-only session cookie after successful login. The cookie is used only to maintain the signed-in session and is not intended for advertising tracking.

Third-party services

The website is hosted on third-party infrastructure and uses an external odds-data provider. Those providers may process technical request information under their own policies.

Analytics

Top Notch Arb does not currently require advertising cookies. If analytics or advertising tools are added later, this policy should be updated before they are enabled.

Data retention

Server and hosting logs may be retained for security, reliability and debugging according to the hosting provider's retention settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page. This policy should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before commercial launch.
